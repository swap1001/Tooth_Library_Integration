The monoblock try-in enables the export of complete denture data for try-in when using closed tooth data.

4. Presets and tooth arrangement system
Should predefined 28-tooth arrangements be used in exocad?
If yes: Are there any requirements or references for the tooth arrangement system?

Presets are predefined arrangements of the upper and lower teeth. They consist of previously integrated tooth sets rearranged for a specific setup, for example with a crossbite or for fully balanced occlusion. The arrangement follows the Gerber concept; arrangements following the Gysi concept are also available on request. These setups save dental technicians time.
